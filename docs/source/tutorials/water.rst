########################
 Your first calculation
########################

The shortest path from a fresh installation to a number. A single water molecule, one
input file, one ``koopmans run``, and the energies of its highest occupied and lowest
unoccupied orbital read off the output. Nothing here needs any prior knowledge of
Koopmans functionals; the tutorials that follow explain what the calculation was
actually doing.
