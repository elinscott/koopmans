#########################################################
 The ionization potential and electron affinity of ozone
#########################################################

A KI calculation on an ozone molecule, with the screening parameters computed from
total-energy differences, ending in an ionization potential and an electron affinity to
compare against experiment. This is the first tutorial where the screening parameters
matter, so it explains what they are and why they have to be calculated rather than
guessed.
